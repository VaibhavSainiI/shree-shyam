Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for all unused items in original condition with tags attached.

How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days, while express delivery takes 2-3 business days.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we ship within India only. International shipping will be available soon.

How can I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. Use it on our website to track your package.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and cash on delivery.

Can I exchange an item for a different size?

Yes, we offer free size exchanges within 30 days of purchase for unused items.
